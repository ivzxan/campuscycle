# CampusCycle

**Pass it on. Find what you need.**

CampusCycle is a campus marketplace for BSIS students and alumni of Dr. Filemon C. Aguilar Memorial College of Las Piñas. It is a simple place to sell, give away, swap, or ask for the things students use every day: books, reviewers, Arduino parts, cables, uniforms, and project materials.

## Why i made it

Every semester, students finish a subject and are left with things they no longer need. The next batch of students ends up buying the same books and components all over again, or going without. The two groups rarely find each other.

CampusCycle connects them. What one student is done with can be exactly what another student needs next.

## What you can do

- **Browse** items from other students. Search by name or subject, and filter by type or category.
- **Post** something in under a minute. Add up to three photos, a price if you are selling, and a short description.
- **Choose how to share it.** Every listing is one of four types:
  - **For sale:** set your own price
  - **Free:** give it to someone who can use it
  - **Swap:** trade for something else
  - **Looking For:** ask the community for something you need
- **Chat** with the person who posted, right inside the app. You can have many conversations going at once, and you do not have to share your phone number.
- **Get alerts** when someone replies to you, or when an item you asked about is gone.
- **Build a profile** with your name, program, a short bio, and an avatar color. Tap any name to see who you are dealing with and what else they have posted.
- **Keep track of your own listings** from your personal dashboard, and mark them done when they are taken.

## Made for phones

Most students will use it on their phones, sometimes on slow connections. CampusCycle is designed to load quickly: photos are shrunk before they upload, and pages only load what they need.

## Staying safe

- Your email address is never shown to other students.
- Conversations are private between the two people in them.
- There are limits on posting and messaging to keep out spam.
- Any listing can be reported, and reported listings can be taken down.
- Payments are not handled in the app. Meet on campus, check the item, and pay in person.

## Who can join

Anyone can browse the listings without an account. To post or send messages, create a free account with a personal email address, so it still works after you graduate. As for now, my target user are BSIS students from DFCAMCLP
